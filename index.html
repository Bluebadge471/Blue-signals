London Session SMC Playbook

A repeatable, rules-based process used by many SMC traders during the highest-volume European window.

1

Mark the Asian Range (Liquidity Map)

Before London opens, identify the high and low of the Asian session (typically 00:00–08:00 London / 19:00–03:00 NY previous day). These levels hold the majority of resting stop orders.

  • Equal highs / equal lows = strongest liquidity pools
  • Previous day high & low also matter
  • Higher-timeframe bias (4H / Daily) filters direction
2

Wait for the London Open Liquidity Sweep

In the first 60–90 minutes after London open (especially the Kill Zone 07:00–10:00), price frequently raids one side of the Asian range — the “Judas swing” or stop hunt.

  • Sweep of Asian low → potential bullish reversal
  • Sweep of Asian high → potential bearish reversal
  • Look for aggressive displacement away from the swept level
3

Confirm with Structure Shift + Imbalance

Do not enter on the sweep alone. Wait for clear evidence that smart money has reversed intent.

  • Market Structure Shift (MSS / CHOCH) on 5m or 15m
  • Break of Structure (BOS) in the new direction
  • Fair Value Gap (FVG) or Order Block created by the displacement
4

Enter, Manage Risk, Target Opposite Liquidity

Enter on the retest of the FVG or Order Block. Stop goes beyond the sweep extreme. First target is often the opposite side of the Asian range or next HTF liquidity.

  • Risk 0.5–1% per trade maximum
  • Partial profits at 1:2 / 1:3 R:R
  • Trail or hold runners into New York if bias aligns

Core SMC Concepts

Quick reference for the language used in this system.

📍 Order Block (OB)

The last opposing candle before a strong impulsive move. Institutions often leave orders here. Price frequently returns to the OB for entry.

⚡ Fair Value Gap (FVG)

A three-candle imbalance where price moved so fast that a gap remains. Price often revisits the FVG to “fill” it before continuing.

🔄 Liquidity Sweep

Price briefly trades beyond equal highs/lows or session extremes to trigger stops, then reverses. The classic London open move against the Asian range.

📈 BOS / CHOCH

Break of Structure continues the current trend. Change of Character (MSS) signals a potential reversal of the immediate structure.

🕰️ Kill Zone

High-probability window when institutions are most active. London Kill Zone ≈ 07:00–10:00 London time. Most clean sweeps and displacements occur here.

🎯 Premium / Discount

In a dealing range, the upper half is premium (sell zone), lower half is discount (buy zone). Prefer entries in the direction of HTF bias inside the correct half.

How to add Asia / London / New York Session Boxes

The free chart widget cannot load custom Pine scripts automatically. Follow these steps (takes ~20 seconds) to get proper session highlighting:

  1. Click the fullscreen button on the chart (or open the same symbol on TradingView.com).
  2. Click Indicators at the top.
  3. Search for one of these free indicators and click it:
Session Range Boxes →
by cfdtools — Clean Asia / London / NY boxes with high-low
Forex Killzones & Sessions →
by QuantyFin — Sessions + ICT Kill Zones (recommended for SMC)
Trading Sessions and Kill Zones →
by allaeuer — Full sessions + London & NY Kill Zones

Tip: After adding the indicator, open its settings and set the timezone to Europe/London (or New York if you prefer). Most of these indicators auto-adjust for daylight saving.
